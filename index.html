AJRA – Privacy Policy

How AJRA handles personal information when you use the application.

Developer: Jean Luc HERINIAINA
Effective date: 16 November 2026
Last updated: 16 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Jean Luc HERINIAINA (“we”, “us” or “our”), the developer of the AJRA mobile application (“AJRA” or the “App”), collects, uses, stores and shares information when you use the App.

Important: AJRA does not provide account registration or user accounts. Some features may nevertheless require access to information or permissions on your device. The App should request access only when needed for the relevant feature, and you can manage permissions in your device settings.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use and the permissions you grant, AJRA may collect or access the following categories of information:

  • Identity and contact information: name, email address and phone number, where you provide them or where they are made available through a feature you use.
  • Profile images and photos: profile photo and images you choose to upload, select or capture using the App.
  • Location information: approximate or precise location, including GPS location, if you grant location permission and use a feature that requires it.
  • Contacts: contact information on your device, only if you grant contacts permission and use a feature that accesses it.
  • Camera: camera access and images captured through the camera when you use a camera-related feature and grant permission.
  • Device information: information such as device model, operating system version, app version, language, identifiers and technical diagnostics, where made available by the device or services used by the App.
  • Usage and analytics information: information about how the App is used, feature interactions, performance, crash reports and diagnostic events, where analytics or diagnostic tools are enabled.

We do not intend to access your contacts, camera, photos or location in the background or without a feature-related reason. You can deny or revoke permissions in your device settings, although some App features may then be unavailable.

2. How We Use Information

We may use the information described above, as relevant to the feature you choose, to:

  • provide and operate the App's features;
  • allow you to provide contact details or images where a feature requires them;
  • provide location-dependent functionality when you enable it;
  • maintain, secure, troubleshoot and improve the App;
  • understand app usage and diagnose errors or performance problems; and
  • respond to privacy requests or support enquiries.

3. Legal Bases and Your Choices

Where applicable law requires a legal basis for processing, we rely on your consent (including device permissions), the need to provide a feature you request, our legitimate interests in maintaining and securing the App, or another lawful basis permitted by applicable law. You may withdraw a device permission at any time through your Android settings. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that took place before withdrawal.

4. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share personal information with third parties for their own independent advertising purposes.

Information may be disclosed only where necessary to operate the App (for example, to a hosting, analytics, crash-reporting or other technical service provider actually used by the App), where you ask us to share it through an App feature, where required by law, or where reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety and security.

Any service provider that processes information on our behalf should be permitted to use it only to provide its services and should be subject to appropriate confidentiality and security obligations. The developer must update this section if the App uses specific third-party SDKs or services that collect or receive data.

5. Data Storage, Retention and Deletion

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, to provide the relevant feature, resolve disputes, meet legal obligations or protect the App. Retention periods depend on the type of information and how the App processes it.

Because AJRA does not offer user accounts, there is no in-app account deletion process. To request access to, correction or deletion of personal information associated with your use of AJRA, email heriniaina.luk@gmail.com. Please describe your request and provide enough information for us to understand it. Do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information. We may need to retain certain information where the law permits or requires it.

Information stored only on your device may be removed by deleting it within the App (if that option exists), clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App. Uninstalling the App may not delete information already transmitted to a server or third-party service.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure. No method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

AJRA is not intended to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us at heriniaina.luk@gmail.com so that we can review the matter and take appropriate action.

8. International Data Transfers

If information is processed or stored in a country other than the country where you live, the data protection laws of that country may differ. Where required, appropriate safeguards will be used for international transfers.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction, deletion or restriction of your personal information, to object to certain processing, or to withdraw consent. To exercise an applicable right, contact us using the details below. You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. If a change is material, we will provide an appropriate notice through the App or another reasonable channel where required.
